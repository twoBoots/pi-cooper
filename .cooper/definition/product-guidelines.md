# Product Guidelines

## Brand Voice & Tone
- **Tone**: Professional, clear, deterministic, and helpful.
- **Prose Style**: Concise and unambiguous. Avoid unnecessary fluff and marketing jargon.

## User Experience Principles
- **Clarity over Cleverness**: Interfaces, error messages, and logs should be straightforward to comprehend.
- **Predictable Behavior**: Commands and workflows should fail gracefully with actionable remediation guidance.
- **Safety First**: Destructive actions must require explicit confirmation.

## Documentation & Markdown Standards
- Use GitHub Flavored Markdown.
- Ensure relative links between documents are valid and clickable.
- Structure complex information with tables and Mermaid diagrams where beneficial.
